# Remote Job Sites

A curated directory of remote-friendly job boards, regional hiring platforms, and career sites for finding fully remote, work-from-home, and location-flexible roles.

This repository is organized by geography first so it is easier to browse:

* Global job sites
* United States
* Canada
* Western Europe
* Eastern Europe
* LATAM
* Asia (Singapore and the Philippines only)

> **Note:** “Remote” does not always mean “work from anywhere.” Always verify hiring location, time-zone expectations, contractor vs. employee status, and work authorization requirements on the original job post.

---

## How to Use This List

* Start with **global remote-first platforms** for broad discovery.
* Then check **regional job boards** that match your target market.
* Set up **saved searches and alerts** on your top platforms.
* Apply through the **official company careers page** when possible.
* Revisit this repository regularly because links, policies, and remote eligibility can change.

## Practical Job Search Tips

### 1. Set alerts everywhere

Create saved searches and job alerts on the 5 to 10 platforms that best match your target market.

### 2. Check location restrictions carefully

Look for terms such as **US only**, **Canada only**, **EU time zones**, **LATAM**, **Singapore-based**, or **Philippines-only**.

### 3. Use boards for discovery, then verify on the company site

Whenever possible, apply through the employer’s official careers page after confirming the role is genuine and still open.

### 4. Track your applications

Maintain a simple spreadsheet or tracker with the role, company, source, location restriction, pay range, application date, and follow-up status.

### 5. Prioritize relevance over volume

A smaller number of high-fit applications usually performs better than mass applying across every board.

### 6. Optimize for remote work

Highlight async communication, documentation, self-management, cross-functional collaboration, and distributed-team experience in your resume and LinkedIn profile.

### 7. Re-check this repository regularly

Remote hiring trends and platform quality change over time. Some boards improve, some decline, and some disappear.
